# China Private-Market Deck Context

## Audience Calibration

Different investors may prioritize different evidence:

- market-oriented VC: category, speed, learning, product-market fit, exit scale;
- growth or PE: repeatability, governance, earnings quality, downside, exit path;
- industrial capital: strategic fit, supply chain, joint commercialization, control;
- state-owned or guidance capital: mandate, location, industrial contribution,
  compliance, governance, and policy fit;
- dual-currency investor: entity structure, cross-border rights, FX, data, and exit.

Do not stereotype a fund. Use its confirmed mandate and participant role.

## Hard-Tech Story

Connect technical proof to customer value and commercialization. Distinguish
prototype, validation, pilot, small-batch, mass production, and scaled delivery.
Show measurable performance, yield, cost, reliability, certification, capacity,
supply dependency, and customer acceptance when material.

Treat `领先`, `唯一`, `国产替代`, and `卡脖子` as management assertions unless the
comparison method and source support them.

## Financial Presentation

- State CNY units explicitly.
- Separate contract, delivery, invoice, revenue recognition, receivable, and cash.
- Identify accounting basis, consolidation scope, audit status, and forecast owner.
- Label subsidies and non-recurring items.
- Tie use of proceeds to measurable milestones, timing, and runway.
- Record valuation date and FX date for cross-currency comparisons.

## Disclosure

Customer names, logos, technical parameters, supply-chain dependencies, personal
data, government relationships, and transaction terms may require recipient-
specific approval. Use generic descriptions or ranges only when the owner approves
the transformation and the result does not permit easy re-identification.
