# China private-markets modeling controls

Record CNY unit scale and every currency conversion, including rate source and rate date. State PRC GAAP, IFRS, US GAAP, management-accounting, and offshore/onshore entity differences instead of collapsing them. Keep PRC tax, VAT, grants, leases, SAFE-related funding constraints, and minority interests as evidenced inputs or approved assumptions.

For A/H/offshore peers and Chinese private rounds, disclose valuation date, listing venue, liquidity differences, instrument terms, pre-money/post-money basis, primary versus secondary capital, and any translation limitation. Treat claims such as “领先”, “唯一”, “国产替代”, and “卡脖子” as management assertions unless primary evidence supports them.
