# Screening method

## Mode depth

### One-minute

Use only high-signal fields available from a BP/teaser/company profile: identity, problem/product, stage, financing ask, traction evidence, team evidence, mandate fit, obvious conflicts, critical unknowns, and next verification. Do not pretend this is diligence.

### Preliminary

Add evidence quality, thesis/counter-thesis, business/technical/financial/governance dimensions, kill criteria, risk carry-forward, and sequenced diligence actions.

### Re-screen

Compare new evidence against the prior screen. Preserve unchanged unknowns and prior severity floors. Explain whether a change reflects new facts, a corrected error, a method change, or a human override.

### GP fit

Compare the company with an identified GP/fund's documented stage, sector, geography, ticket, ownership, portfolio conflicts, fund timing, and process constraints. Do not infer access, interest, or key-person power.

## Evidence grades

Use qualitative grades when useful:

- `A`: source-confirmed primary/official evidence relevant to the exact entity and period;
- `B`: reliable user-provided working evidence or corroborated third-party evidence;
- `C`: single assertion, inference, stale evidence, or incomplete support;
- `U`: unknown, conflicting, or not reviewed.

These grades describe evidence, not investment quality.

## Scoring rules

- Use user-provided weights and thresholds only after `method_assumption_approval`.
- Otherwise present unweighted dimensions or broad bands.
- Show numerator, denominator, excluded dimensions, and unknown treatment.
- Do not use a model score as a formal pass/fail or IC recommendation.
- Do not average incompatible dimensions to conceal a kill criterion.
