# Meeting Recipes

## Recipe: General Pre-Meeting

Lead: `prepare-investment-meeting`.

1. Classify meeting, gate, side, objective, participant authority, and relationship
   owner.
2. Build the evidence brief, hypotheses, objections, agenda, role plan, landmines,
   disclosure boundaries, and capture plan.
3. Obtain relationship-owner approval for sensitive strategy.
4. Hand the approved capture plan to the post-meeting phase if requested.

## Recipe: Diligence Interview

Lead artifact owners remain separate:

1. `prepare-investment-meeting` owns meeting objective, participants, agenda,
   hypotheses, proof references, and logistics.
2. `draft-dd-interview-questions` owns the role-specific question tree, follow-ups,
   evidence requests, red flags, and stop conditions.
3. Human DD owner approves restricted topics and sequencing.
4. `capture-investment-meeting` owns the post-interview record and update proposals.

Do not treat meeting preparation questions as a substitute for a full DD bank.

## Recipe: GP Introduction or Screen

Use confirmed mandate, participant authority, relationship owner, approved company
facts, likely next gate, and disclosure tier. Prepare sponsor-path hypotheses but
do not state that a participant can sponsor, vote, or decide without evidence.
Capture objections, requested evidence, and process signals after the meeting.

## Recipe: Initiation, Internal Review, or IC

Before the meeting, prepare decisive issues, evidence gaps, sensitivities,
conditions, dissent questions, and formal capture roles. After the meeting, mark
minutes `draft_unapproved`; route to designated secretary or chair and counsel when
appropriate. Never infer a vote or approval from discussion tone.

## Recipe: Valuation or TS

Prepare current positions, method and date, amount, dilution, rights, conditions,
exclusivity, fallback positions, decision authority, and restricted topics. Capture
who stated each position, what was agreed, conditions, open terms, and follow-up.
Do not convert negotiation discussion into final legal terms.

## Recipe: Portfolio or Board

Prepare operating performance, cash, forecast, risk, governance decisions, and
requested support. Separate board materials from informal portfolio discussion.
Formal minutes require the designated governance process; the capture atom only
produces a draft record and proposals.

## Recipe: Internal FA Debrief

Capture facts learned, relationship or process signals, objections, blockers,
promised items, owners, and next actions. Label interpretations as hypotheses.
Propose updates to the deal record or institutional memory but do not write them.

## Recipe: Digital-Persona Context Pack

Use only when a user explicitly requests a context pack for a GP key person,
investment professional, FA relationship owner, or institutional role.

1. `prepare-investment-meeting` contributes verified identity, role, authority
   basis, approved side, project scope, current objectives, and source-specific
   context.
2. `capture-investment-meeting` contributes only human-validated fact, preference,
   question, and process update proposals. Exclude unapproved inference and do not
   mutate a memory store.
3. A deterministic assembler separates `public`, `confidential`, `prohibited`, and
   `human_escalation_required` fields and records source, version, reviewer, and
   expiry for every material context block.
4. The authorized person or institutional owner approves identity, preferences,
   permitted representation, accessible projects, recipient scope, and expiry.

The pack must define refusal rules for unsupported facts, personal intent,
confidential matters, investment commitments, legal interpretations, sensitive
disclosures, and any answer outside the authorized side. It is contextual input,
not authority to impersonate the person, vote, negotiate, promise, or disclose.

## Failure and Fallback

Stop when consent is unresolved for a recording, participant identity is too weak,
the formal record owner is unknown, or restricted sources exceed approved scope.
When transcription or diarization is unavailable, return a structured note template
or partial minutes with coverage gaps.
