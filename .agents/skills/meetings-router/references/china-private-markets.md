## Participant Authority

GP founder, investment/business partner, sector head, MD/ED/VP, investment
director, manager, legal, finance, and post-investment roles vary by institution.
Map execution ability, sponsorship, vote, and decision authority independently.
Small firms may concentrate roles; large firms may separate initiation, internal
review, DD, and IC responsibilities. Confirm rather than assume.

## Process Stages

Common gates include contact, NDA, information request, research, initiation,
internal review, business DD, FDD/LDD, TS, investment recommendation, IC,
definitive agreement, closing, and post-investment. The same participants may
behave differently across gates; classify each meeting from its actual objective.

## Governance and Sensitivity

Board, shareholder, IC, state-owned, guidance-fund, and regulated processes may
require specific voting, seal, signature, privilege, retention, and minute controls.
Use qualified human owners. Keep personal, relationship, internal GP, customer,
technical, government, state-owned, legal, and unannounced transaction information
within approved access boundaries.
