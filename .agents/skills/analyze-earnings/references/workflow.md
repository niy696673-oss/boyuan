# Earnings-analysis workflow

## Build the source package

Capture the release, filing, official presentation, call/transcript, prior guidance,
dated consensus, prior internal estimates, market reaction, and relevant sector sources.
Record unavailable items and extraction coverage.

## Sequence

1. Confirm company, period, event time, currency, accounting basis, and target linkage.
2. Create the source register before extracting figures.
3. Reconcile headline figures across official materials.
4. Build actual/consensus/prior/guidance comparisons with source dates.
5. Analyze operating, margin, cash, balance-sheet, segment, KPI, and guidance drivers.
6. Separate reported results from one-time, adjusted, and inferred components.
7. Identify direct relationships and weaker sector read-throughs.
8. Map evidence to private-company thesis pillars and decisions.
9. Obtain analyst approval for material interpretations.
10. Propose follow-up events without mutating another system.

## Stop conditions

Do not publish a complete conclusion when the filing/release is unavailable, the period
cannot be confirmed, material units conflict, or the purported consensus has no dated
source. Deliver a partial-analysis status and explicit source requests.

## Handoff

Send `monitor-catalysts` only proposed events with source, verification status, owner,
next-check date, and action gate. Send model workflows only approved estimate changes.
