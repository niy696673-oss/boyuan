# Earnings-analysis financial methods

## Variance

```text
AbsoluteVariance = Actual - Comparison
PercentageVariance = (Actual - Comparison) / abs(Comparison)
```

Use percentage variance only when the denominator is meaningful. Label `NM` for negative,
near-zero, or basis-incompatible comparisons.

## Period discipline

Distinguish quarter-only, year-to-date, LTM, fiscal year, and calendar year. When deriving a
quarter from cumulative disclosures, show the subtraction and preserve both source values.

## Profit and margin

- Gross margin = gross profit / revenue.
- Operating margin uses the explicitly defined operating-profit numerator.
- Separate total, parent-attributable, recurring, adjusted, and non-GAAP profit.
- Reconcile EPS to the applicable diluted share count and period.

## Cash conversion

Define operating cash flow and CapEx consistently before computing free cash flow. Examine
receivables, inventory, payables, contract balances, provisions, taxes, and non-cash items.

## Consensus and guidance

Consensus requires a named source and as-of date. A model estimate is not consensus.
Map management guidance to the period it actually covers and preserve ranges.

## Read-through confidence

Classify direct disclosed relationships separately from inferred sector signals. Support
material private-company implications with a causal chain and counter-evidence.
