# China earnings and private-market overlay

- Verify the applicable exchange/board and current disclosure rules at runtime.
- Use official exchange/CNINFO filings, company IR materials, results-meeting records, and
  other primary sources before news or broker summaries.
- Distinguish 归母净利润, 扣非归母净利润, total net income, government grants, other income,
  fair-value changes, impairment, and share-based payment.
- Preserve cumulative vs quarter-only disclosure and CNY unit scale.
- Do not assume a universal earnings-preview threshold, release deadline, call schedule,
  price limit, or Northbound-flow data field.
- Treat broker consensus and commercial data as licensed, dated sources; do not reproduce
  paywalled content beyond permitted use.
- Map public earnings to private targets through customer demand, pricing, supplier orders,
  capacity, channel inventory, regulation, competitive financing, valuation and exit windows.
- Label statements such as `国产替代`, `技术突破`, `行业第一`, or `唯一` as management claims
  unless independent evidence supports them.
- Do not produce a public-stock rating or target price unless a separate explicit mandate
  and professional review allow it.
