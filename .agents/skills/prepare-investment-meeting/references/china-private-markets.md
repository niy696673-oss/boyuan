# China Private-Market Meeting Context

## Meeting-Type Adaptation

| Meeting type | Primary preparation focus |
|---|---|
| Founder discovery | founder judgment, ambition, truthfulness, priorities, financing readiness |
| BP coaching | claim quality, narrative gaps, proof plan, founder approvals |
| GP introduction | mandate fit, sponsor path, trust, approved disclosure, next gate |
| Investment-manager screen | one-minute logic, rejection risks, partner-sponsor route |
| Initiation or internal review | evidence sufficiency, open issues, internal owners, meeting decision |
| IC or investment committee | decisive issues, sensitivities, mitigants, unresolved dissent, governance |
| Management DD | business, organization, strategy, execution, customer and operating proof |
| Customer/reference DD | relationship, selection criteria, use, alternatives, renewal, permission |
| Expert/technical DD | test method, benchmarks, maturity, IP, manufacturability, failure modes |
| FDD/LDD coordination | workstream scope, request ownership, conflicts, schedule, privilege |
| Valuation/TS | valuation basis, amount, dilution, rights, conditions, exclusivity, fallback |
| LP/fundraising | strategy, team, track record, governance, portfolio, mandate, alignment |
| Portfolio/board | operating performance, cash, risks, decisions, governance, support |
| Exit/follow-on | readiness, buyer/investor logic, timing, valuation, process, disclosure |
| Internal FA debrief | facts learned, relationship signals, blockers, owner, next action |

## GP Participant Roles

In China private-market firms, useful roles may include founder, investment or
business partner, sector head, MD/ED/VP, investment director, manager, legal,
finance, or post-investment lead. Title conventions vary. Confirm who can execute,
sponsor, vote, and decide rather than relying on title alone.

## Process Context

Possible gates include project contact, NDA, information request, research,
initiation, internal review, business DD, FDD/LDD, TS, investment recommendation,
IC, definitive agreement, closing, and post-investment. Adapt depth and disclosure
to the immediate gate.

## Sensitive Topics

Relationship notes, internal GP views, government or state-owned relationships,
customer names, personal data, transaction terms, legal issues, and technical trade
secrets require explicit scope and access control. Do not place them in public
examples, reusable tests, or broad-distribution agendas.
