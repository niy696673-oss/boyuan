# China Private-Market Minutes Context

## Meeting-Type Emphasis

| Meeting type | Capture emphasis |
|---|---|
| Founder discovery | founder statements, judgment, priorities, unresolved credibility tests |
| GP introduction or screen | mandate fit, sponsor path, objections, requested evidence, next gate |
| Initiation/internal review | decision, conditions, owners, dissent, evidence gaps |
| IC/investment committee | formal decision language, conditions, vote/dissent only as validated by secretary or chair |
| Management DD | assertion, proof offered, missing proof, follow-up owner |
| Customer/reference DD | permission, relationship basis, use, alternatives, selection, renewal, caveats |
| Expert/technical DD | method, benchmark, maturity, failure modes, uncertainty, conflicts |
| FDD/LDD coordination | request, scope, owner, privilege, conflict, timetable |
| Valuation/TS | position, rationale, concession, unresolved term, authority, condition |
| LP/fundraising | mandate, track record claims, team, governance, next diligence step |
| Portfolio/board | performance, cash, risk, decision, governance, owner, due date |
| Internal FA debrief | facts learned, hypotheses, relationship signal, blocker, next action |

## Process and Truth Status

An investor's pause, objection, or positive comment is not a final decision unless
the authorized decision-maker states and validates it. A participant's title does
not by itself prove voting power. Record the observed process signal and its source
without upgrading it to institutional intent.

## Formal Records

Chinese board, shareholder, IC, state-owned, guidance-fund, or regulated processes
may impose specific minute, voting, seal, signature, privilege, and retention
requirements. This skill does not determine legal sufficiency. The designated
secretary, chair, compliance function, or counsel must control the official record.

## Sensitive Data

Apply strict access controls to personal information, relationship assessments,
government or state-owned relationships, customer references, trade secrets,
unannounced terms, legal advice, and internal GP deliberations. Do not reuse such
content in public examples, evaluation data, or broad institutional memory.
