# Detailed Workflow

## 1. Register Sources

Record each audio file, transcript, note, chat, agenda, and attachment with version,
owner, date, access class, coverage, and reliability. Keep human notes distinct
from machine transcription. Do not merge conflicting sources silently.

## 2. Establish Participants

Map diarization labels to people only with sufficient evidence. Preserve `unknown`
speakers. Record joins, exits, partial attendance, interpreter involvement, and
speaker-confidence changes when material.

## 3. Segment and Extract

Segment by topic and material event, not arbitrary transcript length. Capture:

- material company or market assertions;
- decisions and decision conditions;
- commitments and who made them;
- actions, owners, dates, dependencies, and recipients;
- questions and promised follow-up evidence;
- objections, risks, conflicts, and dissent;
- process signals and next-gate expectations.

## 4. Classify and Link

For each event, record type, evidence state, speaker, exact provenance, confidence,
and access class. A confident transcript supports what was said, not whether the
statement is independently true.

## 5. Draft by Meeting Type

Adapt emphasis without changing the record. A founder meeting may emphasize
judgment and strategy; management DD may emphasize claims and proof; TS meetings
may emphasize positions and open terms; board or IC records require formal decision,
dissent, abstention, condition, and governance handling by designated humans.

## 6. Build Update Proposals

Compare new events to the authorized prior state. Propose changes with before
state, evidence, conflict, validator, target system, and access class. Keep all
mutation status `not_performed`.

## 7. Validate

Ask the meeting owner to validate speakers, substance, decisions, commitments,
actions, changed facts, and sensitivity. Obtain secretary/chair/counsel review for
formal records. Record approved redactions and version history.
