# Role playbooks

Load only the section matching the respondent. These are question dimensions, not presumed findings.

## Founder and management

Test strategic choices, resource allocation, milestone history, forecast construction, governance, financing use, team gaps, and responses to setbacks. Ask for concrete decisions and contemporaneous evidence.

## Finance and board-secretary team

Test metric definitions, entity/period scope, revenue and cost bridges, cash, working capital, tax, related parties, prior financing obligations, controls, forecast ownership, and reconciliation to source records.

## Technical and R&D leaders

Test architecture, technical novelty, reproducibility, performance benchmarks, failure modes, dependencies, roadmap, engineering-to-production transfer, IP contribution/assignment, and evidence that a qualified reviewer can reproduce.

## Sales, customers, and channels

Test procurement process, decision criteria, implementation, usage, renewal, price, alternatives, switching, ROI, pipeline definition, concentration, and exceptions. Avoid requesting another party's confidential pricing or inside information.

## Suppliers and operations

Test qualification, capacity, lead time, yield, quality, single-source exposure, change control, payment terms, traceability, and recovery from disruption.

## Independent experts

Test the expert's competence, conflicts, information basis, market/technology structure, substitutes, adoption constraints, and falsifiable indicators. Do not let the expert opine beyond their scope.

## Employees and former employees

Confirm consent and lawful outreach first. Focus on first-hand processes, role boundaries, organizational change, controls, and observed examples. Do not solicit confidential documents, personal grievances as fact, or privileged communications.
