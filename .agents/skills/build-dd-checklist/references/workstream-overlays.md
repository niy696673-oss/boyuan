# Workstream overlays

Apply the baseline, then add only overlays supported by the deal facts. Treat this as a request taxonomy, not a statement of law or a finding.

## Baseline

- Corporate history, registration records, actual controller, cap table, shareholder rights, nominee/entrustment arrangements, prior financings, ESOP, related parties, governance, and disputes.
- Products, customers, pricing, pipeline, contracts, suppliers, capacity, quality, delivery, competition, forecasts, and operating plan.
- Historical financial statements, ledgers, revenue/cost bridges, working capital, debt, cash, capex, tax, related-party balances, and forecast support.
- Core team, employment/consulting arrangements, incentives, social insurance, key-person dependency, and departures.
- IP ownership and assignment chain, licenses, permits, data/security controls, insurance, EHS/ESG, and transaction approvals.

## Stage overlays

- **Early stage:** founder commitments, technical proof, IP chain, runway, milestone financing, customer validation, and cap-table hygiene.
- **Growth stage:** revenue quality, cohort/unit economics, channel dependence, working capital, capacity expansion, internal controls, and forecast reliability.
- **Buyout/control:** debt capacity, change-of-control, cash conversion, management incentives, separation, covenant inputs, and exit readiness.
- **Follow-on/exit:** prior investor rights, consent, historical use of proceeds, KPI delivery, readiness gaps, and market-window dependencies.

## Sector overlays

- **Hard technology:** technical architecture, reproducibility, performance benchmarks, engineering samples, certification, yield, reliability, roadmap, and IP freedom-to-operate inputs.
- **Advanced manufacturing:** BOM, capacity, utilization, yield, scrap, quality systems, equipment, maintenance, supply concentration, traceability, and capex.
- **Military-adjacent/export-sensitive:** business classification, customer/end-use representations, controlled technology/data, licenses, classifications, geographic exposure, and specialist review. Request evidence; do not decide legal status.
- **Data-intensive:** personal/important data inventory, consent/legal-basis records, access controls, incident logs, vendors, localization/cross-border flows, and specialist review.

## Professional ownership

Label each request as investment-team, technical, FDD, LDD, tax, cybersecurity, EHS, or another specialist workstream. The checklist may coordinate scopes but must not merge professional conclusions.
