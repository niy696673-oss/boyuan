# China Private-Market Artifact Map

## Artifact Boundaries

| Artifact | Primary owner | Typical audience | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| BP diagnostic | `diagnose-bp` | Founder and FA | Gaps and remediation, no rewrite or investment decision |
| BP rewrite | `rewrite-bp` | Founder, FA, selected investor audience | Editorial transformation, no format ownership |
| Pitch deck | `build-pitch-deck` | Investor meeting | Presentation story and QA |
| Teaser | `build-teaser` | Controlled outreach | Minimal disclosure and CTA |
| CIM | `build-cim` | Approved investor, usually post-NDA | Detailed company-side financing memorandum |
| QR | `qr_assembler` | FA or investment team | Deterministic composition of approved atomic outputs |
| Initiation report | `initiation_assembler` | Institution's internal gate | Fixed institution template; human decision |
| IC memo | `ic_assembler` | Investment committee | Fixed institution template; human judgment and decision |

## Process Calibration

Common process stages include contact, NDA, information request, research,
initiation, internal review, business DD, FDD/LDD, TS, investment recommendation,
IC, definitive agreement, closing, and post-investment. Route the artifact to the
next legitimate gate; do not make an early outreach document perform late-stage DD.

## Audience Calibration

Company/founder materials emphasize truthful proof and financing milestones. FA
materials translate between company and capital-market language while preserving
source ownership. GP materials support screening, diligence, valuation, and
decision processes. LP materials concern fund strategy, team, track record,
governance, and alignment. Do not blend sides without labeling voice and access.

## China-Specific Controls

- Record exact legal and operating entities.
- State CNY units, accounting basis, audit status, valuation date, and FX date.
- Separate contract, delivery, revenue, receivable, collection, pipeline, and
  forecast.
- Treat government, state-owned, customer, technical, personal, and relationship
  information as potentially restricted.
- Route legal, tax, accounting, regulatory, data, export-control, and state-asset
  conclusions to qualified human reviewers.
