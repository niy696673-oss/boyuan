# China private-market catalyst overlay

Potential categories include:

- exchange/CNINFO filings, results meetings, lock-ups, issuance, M&A, refinancing and IPO;
- NDRC, SAMR, CSRC, CAC, MIIT, NMPA, tax, customs, export-control, data/security and
  sector-regulator events;
- government procurement, tenders, subsidies, industrial policy and standards;
- customer acceptance, distributor inventory, supplier capacity, certification,
  production ramp, quality incidents, clinical milestones and product registration;
- domestic and cross-border fundraising, SAFE/FX, red-chip/VIE restructuring and exit windows.

Controls:

- Verify dates and current rules from primary sources; do not hardcode recurring policy,
  filing, lock-up, or disclosure schedules.
- Distinguish announced policy, draft consultation, effective rule, local implementation,
  rumor, and expert interpretation.
- Respect confidentiality and permissions for private investor/customer/GP information.
- Do not infer that government policy or a named relationship guarantees revenue or financing.
- Record CNY units, jurisdictions, entity scope, and unofficial translations.
- Treat `国产替代`, `自主可控`, `卡脖子`, and similar labels as assertions until evidenced.
- For private companies, prioritize decision relevance over expected public-stock reaction.
