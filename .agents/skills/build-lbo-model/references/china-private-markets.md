# China private-market LBO overlay

- Confirm transaction form, control level, acquisition perimeter, onshore/offshore
  entities, currency, approval path, and financing jurisdiction.
- Treat bank debt, shareholder loans, seller notes, mezzanine, bonds, convertibles,
  guarantees, security, and cross-border funding as evidence-dependent alternatives.
- Do not hardcode leverage, control premium, coupon, LPR spread, amortization, fees,
  covenant, stamp tax, deed tax, or withholding rates.
- Obtain current legal/tax advice for merger control, tender offers, foreign investment,
  industry access, data/security review, FX, tax, security perfection, and distributions.
- Identify cash trapped by entity, restricted cash, related-party balances, entrusted
  loans, shareholder loans, and guarantees.
- Reconcile management rollover, founder shares, employee options, preferred rights,
  redemption, earn-outs, seller financing, and minority interests.
- Distinguish PRC GAAP, IFRS, and management-account forecasts and document adjustments.
- Model CNY and foreign-currency debt separately with dated FX assumptions.
- For ordinary minority growth financing, fail the LBO suitability gate rather than
  presenting hypothetical leverage as an executable transaction.
