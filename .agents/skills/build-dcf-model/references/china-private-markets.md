# China private-market DCF overlay

- State whether the records use PRC GAAP, IFRS, another basis, or management accounts.
- Preserve CNY units exactly; record every conversion among yuan, thousand yuan,
  ten-thousand yuan, and hundred-million yuan.
- Source the CNY risk-free rate, ERP, debt cost, tax status, and exchange rate as of the
  valuation date. Do not reuse static China-market percentages.
- Confirm ordinary/high-and-new-technology-enterprise tax status, loss carryforwards,
  grants, VAT presentation, and withholding only with current evidence and professional input.
- Separate research expense from qualifying development capitalization under the
  applicable accounting policy.
- Treat government grants, related-party balances, shareholder loans, entrusted loans,
  restricted cash, wealth-management products, and non-operating investments explicitly.
- Reconcile registered capital, paid-in capital, preferred rights, employee options,
  convertibles, redemption rights, anti-dilution, and minority interests before per-share value.
- For red-chip, VIE, offshore-holding, or cross-border structures, identify the valued
  entity and currency. Do not assume cash is freely distributable across entities.
- Treat claims such as `领先`, `唯一`, `国产替代`, or `卡脖子` as management assertions
  until supported by evidence.
- For an early-stage or binary milestone company, prefer milestone, financing-round,
  scenario, or comparable methods unless the DCF suitability gate is genuinely passed.
