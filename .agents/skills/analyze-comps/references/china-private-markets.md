# China private-market comparable overlay

- Distinguish A-share, H-share, US-listed China, red-chip, VIE, offshore holding, and
  private-company entity perimeters.
- Record total vs free-float market capitalization and do not mix them.
- Align PRC GAAP, IFRS, US GAAP, and management-account metrics before comparison.
- Use 归母净利润/归母权益 where appropriate and examine 扣非 metrics, grants, impairments,
  related parties, and non-operating items.
- Record CNY unit scale and dated FX for all conversions.
- Treat A/H premiums, liquidity, lock-ups, state ownership, control, instrument rights,
  and foreign-ownership constraints as explainable differences, not fixed discounts.
- For private rounds, verify pre/post-money, financing amount, primary/secondary split,
  preferred terms, redemption, liquidation preference, anti-dilution, convertibles, and date.
- Do not infer round valuation from media language when source terms are incomplete.
- Do not use static industry-premium tables or unverified claims such as `国产替代` to
  justify a premium.
