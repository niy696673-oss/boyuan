# China Private-Market Narrative Context

Apply these principles when relevant; do not treat them as a substitute for
company-specific evidence.

## Investor Language

- Prefer precise descriptions of product, customer value, economics, and proof
  over policy slogans or unsupported category leadership.
- Translate technical differentiation into measurable performance, cost,
  reliability, qualification, delivery, and switching-barrier evidence.
- Connect financing proceeds to operational milestones and the next financing or
  self-funding threshold.
- Distinguish the company story from an investor's independent thesis.

## Stage Calibration

- Early stage: founder-market fit, technical feasibility, initial validation,
  learning speed, and milestone use of proceeds.
- Growth stage: repeatability, revenue quality, unit economics, organization,
  capacity, working capital, and path to durable scale.
- Pre-IPO or later stage: governance, auditability, compliance, related parties,
  concentration, earnings quality, and capital-markets readiness.

## Hard-Tech Wording

Distinguish laboratory result, prototype, engineering sample, pilot line, customer
validation, small-batch delivery, and mass production. State measurement method
and comparison basis for performance claims. Keep `领先`, `唯一`, `国产替代`, and
`卡脖子` labeled as management assertions unless independently supported.

## China-Specific Data Discipline

- Write CNY units explicitly and consistently.
- Identify PRC GAAP, IFRS, management accounts, tax accounts, or another basis.
- Separate contract amount, delivered amount, recognized revenue, invoiced amount,
  cash collection, and pipeline.
- Record valuation and FX dates for converted figures.
- Label unofficial translations and preserve original defined terms where needed.

## Disclosure and Compliance

Do not make legal conclusions. Flag permissions, customer naming, personal data,
state secrets, trade secrets, export controls, data transfer, IP ownership,
regulated production, environmental, and state-asset matters for qualified human
review. Respect NDA and named-recipient restrictions in every variant.
