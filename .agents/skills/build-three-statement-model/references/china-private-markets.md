# China private-company accounting overlay

- Confirm PRC GAAP, IFRS, tax-basis, cash-basis, or management-account source before mapping.
- Preserve Chinese source labels and maintain a bilingual mapping table.
- Record unit scale for yuan, thousand yuan, ten-thousand yuan, and hundred-million yuan.
- Treat revenue as reported under the applicable policy; do not mechanically add or remove VAT.
- Separate research expense from qualifying development capitalization according to the
  approved accounting policy.
- Model government grants, other income, asset/credit impairment, contract assets and
  liabilities, notes receivable/payable, related parties, wealth-management products,
  entrusted loans, restricted cash, construction in progress, and land-use rights when material.
- Reconcile parent-attributable profit/equity and non-controlling interests.
- Confirm enterprise tax status, deferred taxes, loss carryforwards, withholding, and
  distribution constraints with finance and tax owners.
- Use monthly runway and financing schedules for early-stage companies when annual
  three-statement detail creates false precision.
- Never convert management assertions or unaudited schedules into audited facts.
