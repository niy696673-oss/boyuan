# China Private-Market Teaser Context

## Common Sensitive Elements

Treat the following as potentially identifying or restricted: customer names and
logos, industrial-park location, government or state-owned-enterprise relationships,
rare certification combinations, technical parameters, founder employment history,
exclusive suppliers, production capacity, current investors, valuation, subsidy
amounts, and transaction timetable.

## Claim Discipline

- Distinguish signed contract, purchase order, delivery, acceptance, recognized
  revenue, receivable, collection, pipeline, and target.
- Distinguish prototype, pilot, small-batch, mass-production, and scaled-delivery
  status.
- Treat `领先`, `唯一`, `国产替代`, and `卡脖子` as management assertions unless
  supported by an approved method and evidence.
- State CNY units, period, accounting basis, and forecast status.

## Recipient Differences

Government guidance funds, industrial capital, market-oriented VC, growth equity,
and dual-currency funds may respond to different evidence. Select emphasis only
from a confirmed mandate; do not disclose sensitive location, policy, or supply-
chain facts merely to mirror presumed preferences.

## Legal and Process Boundaries

A teaser is not an offering document, legal opinion, due-diligence report, or
guarantee. Route questions about securities distribution, personal data, state
secrets, trade secrets, export controls, data transfer, and disclosure obligations
to qualified counsel. Preserve NDA and recipient restrictions.
