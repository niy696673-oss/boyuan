# China private-markets monitoring controls

Record the jurisdiction, source authority, publication date, time zone, and translation limitation for Chinese policy, exchange, procurement, financing, IPO, export-control, data-security, cybersecurity, antitrust, tax, subsidy, and litigation signals. Treat informal posts, secondary news, historical cadence, and management claims as `inferred` or `user-provided` unless a primary source confirms them.

For PRC issuers, A/H/offshore comparables, and China private companies, retain the disclosure venue, accounting basis, CNY unit scale, exchange-rate date, instrument terms, and onshore/offshore entity relationship. Confidential relationship, key-person, and meeting data require consent and scope controls and must not be exposed as public examples or test fixtures.
