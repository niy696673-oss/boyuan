# China Private-Market Review Context

Use these lenses only when relevant to the company, round, and investor mandate.
They are prompts for evidence, not universal scoring rules.

## Financing Narrative

- Clarify legal entity, operating entity, group structure, and financing entity.
- State round name, security type, target amount, currency, valuation basis,
  existing shareholders, intended proceeds, runway, and next value inflection.
- Distinguish RMB-fund expectations from offshore or dual-currency processes.
- Treat government guidance funds, state-owned capital, industrial capital, and
  market-oriented funds as different audiences with different mandates.

## Hard-Tech Proof

Test technology claims through maturity, measurable performance, validation body,
certification, yield, cost curve, capacity, supply-chain dependency, intellectual
property ownership, and customer adoption. Separate prototype, pilot, small-batch,
mass-production, and scaled-delivery status.

Terms such as `领先`, `唯一`, `国产替代`, and `卡脖子` remain management assertions
unless supported by a named method and credible evidence.

## Commercialization

Separate signed contracts, framework agreements, purchase orders, paid pilots,
unpaid trials, pipeline, and management targets. Explain customer concentration,
sales cycle, acceptance milestones, pricing, unit economics, renewal or repeat
orders, receivables, and revenue-recognition basis.

## Financial and Regulatory Context

- Record CNY units explicitly, including whether a table uses yuan, ten-thousand
  yuan, or one-hundred-million yuan.
- State accounting basis, audit status, consolidation scope, tax treatment, and
  forecast ownership.
- Record valuation date, FX date, FX source, and conversion method.
- Mark unofficial translations as unofficial.
- Flag, without giving legal advice, material permits, data, export-control,
  foreign-investment, securities, environmental, production-safety, IP, and
  state-asset issues for qualified review.

## Investor Process Fit

Assess whether the BP supports the immediate gate: one-minute screen, NDA follow-
up, initiation, business DD, TS negotiation, IC review, or closing preparation.
Do not demand late-stage diligence depth from an early screen, but expose evidence
that must exist before costs are committed.
