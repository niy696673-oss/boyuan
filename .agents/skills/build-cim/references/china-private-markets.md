# China Private-Market CIM Context

## Entity and Transaction

State the financing entity, operating entities, group structure, registered
capital, historical equity changes, current capitalization, proposed security,
currency, amount, valuation basis, proceeds, and process stage. Distinguish onshore,
offshore, red-chip, VIE, and dual-currency structures only from reliable sources.

## Business and Technology

For hard-tech companies, separate research result, prototype, pilot, customer
validation, small-batch, mass production, and scaled delivery. Cover measurable
performance, yield, cost, capacity, certification, IP ownership, supply dependence,
and commercialization evidence. Unsupported `领先`, `唯一`, `国产替代`, and `卡脖子`
claims remain management assertions.

## Financials

- State CNY unit, accounting basis, audit status, consolidation scope, tax basis,
  forecast owner, and forecast date.
- Reconcile contracts, deliveries, revenue recognition, invoices, receivables,
  collections, subsidies, and non-recurring items.
- Explain working capital, capacity investment, capital expenditure, and runway.
- Record valuation date, FX date, FX source, and conversion method.

## Risk and Review Areas

Flag relevant corporate history, related parties, employee incentives, IP,
permits, data, cybersecurity, export control, foreign investment, state assets,
environmental matters, production safety, tax, labor, litigation, customer and
supplier concentration, and transaction terms for qualified review. Do not make
legal or accounting conclusions.

## Distribution

Separate internal, pre-NDA, post-NDA, named-recipient, and restricted-appendix
content. Record customer-name permission, personal-data handling, translation
status, and source-access limitations. A detailed CIM does not remove the need for
DD or independent investor analysis.
